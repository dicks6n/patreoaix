Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated {{ updated }}

Subscriptions (Pro / VIP)

  • Plans give access for the period shown when you pay and are not renewed automatically unless the page says so.
  • Because signals and tools are available immediately, payments are not refundable once the plan has started, except where the law requires or the service was unavailable for a long period through our fault.
  • The free trial ends automatically and is never charged.

Funded challenge fees

  • A challenge fee is not refundable once any trade has been placed on the account, or after the account was breached or passed.
  • An account with no trades can be refunded to your wallet if you ask support within 14 days of buying it.
  • Bundle discounts are calculated on the whole order; refunding part of a bundle refunds the price actually paid per account.

Deposits and withdrawals

  • Crypto deposits are credited after network confirmation and cannot be reversed on the blockchain. A deposit sent to the wrong network or address may be lost.
  • Withdrawals require verified identity. We may hold a withdrawal while we check it (see the AML policy). A first-deposit bonus stays locked until its trading volume is reached; your own deposit and profits stay withdrawable.
  • Transfers between PATREOAIX users are instant and cannot be reversed by us except for fraud or error that we can prove.

P2P trades

Completed P2P trades are final. Disputes are handled through the in-order appeal process described in the P2P rules.
