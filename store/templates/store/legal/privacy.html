Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

This policy explains what PATREOAIX ({{ domain }}) collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored hashed), and login records used to keep your account secure.
  • Trading and payment records: deposits, withdrawals, orders and balances on the platform, and the payment references our payment providers send us.
  • Identity verification: your ID document and a selfie, used only to verify your identity and meet legal duties. Automatic checks are done by our processor Didit (didit.me), which compares your selfie with your ID photo (biometric face matching) and checks the document; we receive the result and basic document details (name, document type and number), not your images.
  • Connected accounts: if you connect a broker or social account, the access tokens needed to provide that feature.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and pages visited, used for security and to keep the service working.

How we use it

  • To run your account, process payments and show your trading activity.
  • To send the notifications and signals you asked for.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to meet legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal data.

TikTok and other social platforms

PATREOAIX uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API only for the PATREOAIX account owner, to publish our own AI signal images to our own TikTok account. We receive the basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) and an access token, which are stored on our server and used only to publish those posts. We do not access or store other TikTok users' data. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings (Manage app permissions) or from our site.

Wallet, transfers and P2P

We record deposits, withdrawals, transfers and P2P orders. When you transfer money, the recipient sees your username and a shortened name. In a P2P order, the other trader sees your username, the payment details you chose to share for that order, and the messages and proof photos in the order chat; support can see them when an appeal is opened. Proof photos are only shown to the two traders and support.

Notifications

If you turn on push notifications we store your browser's push address to send you alerts; turning notifications off deletes it.

Cookies

See the Cookie Policy. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Sharing

We share data only with the services needed to run PATREOAIX (hosting, email, payment processors and connected brokers you choose), or when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep data while your account is open and as long as the law requires afterwards. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed and access to stored data is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
