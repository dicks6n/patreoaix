Objectives

  • Profit target: reach it on closed trades with no positions open.
  • Minimum trading days: trade on at least 3 different days in each phase.
  • No time limit. Leverage 1:{{ prop_leverage }}.
  • Passing a phase opens the next phase (or the funded account) with a new account number.

Loss limits (breach = account closed)

  • Daily loss: equity may not fall more than the daily % of the starting balance below the day's starting level (the higher of balance and equity at 00:00 UTC).
  • Maximum loss: equity may not fall below the starting balance minus the max %.
  • Limits count floating losses. On a breach all positions are closed and the account ends; the fee is not refunded.

Payouts

  • Funded accounts can request a payout every {{ prop_payout_days }} days, with at least ${{ prop_payout_min }} profit and no open positions.
  • Identity verification (KYC) is required before the first payout.
  • The payout is your split of the profit; the account balance then resets to its starting size. Payouts are reviewed and credited to your wallet.

Not allowed

  • Exploiting price errors, delays or feed glitches; hedging between your own or other people's accounts; account sharing or trading someone else's account.
  • Anything that is not genuine trading. We may cancel profits, payouts and accounts that break these rules.
