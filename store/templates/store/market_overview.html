How the coach decides

Trends move in waves: a strong push (the impulse), then a pullback (retracement) before the next push. Buying at the top of an impulse means a wide stop and little reward. Buying after a pullback gives a tight stop and the old high as a target.

  • Trend: the main chart must make higher highs and higher lows (or lower highs and lower lows). Swings must be at least 2 ATR to count, so noise is ignored. The higher timeframe should agree.
  • Where: the pullback is measured with Fibonacci on the last leg. 0–23.6% = still at the extreme, don't chase. 38.2–78.6% = entry zone, 50–61.8% = golden zone. Beyond 100% = the trend is broken.
  • Why there: the zone is stronger when support/resistance, an old swing level or an EMA sits inside it.
  • When: price must show it is turning: a close beyond the pullback's last lower high (higher low for sells), a rejection candle in the zone, or a close back over the 20 EMA. Only closed candles count.
  • Risk: stop beyond the pullback extreme plus 0.25 ATR, target the old high/low then the 127% and 161.8% extensions. Below 1.5R the coach says no.
  • Right now: no trade into high-impact news, in a dead session, with a wide spread, or when you already hold the market.
