Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free; your plan (Starter, Pro or VIP) is the same as on the website.

Why isn't it on the Play Store yet?

It's distributed directly from patreoaix.com for now. Updates are announced in the app and on our Telegram channel; download the new APK and install it over the old one, your login stays.

How often do notifications arrive?

The Android app checks for new notifications every 15 minutes and as soon as you open it. On Chrome or the Home Screen app, notifications arrive instantly.

Does it use a lot of data or battery?

No. It's a light app (under 100 KB) and the background check is a tiny request every 15 minutes.
